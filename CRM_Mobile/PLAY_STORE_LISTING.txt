App name:
Trackbook CRM

Short description (max 80 characters):
Field CRM for Manager, TL & BDM — leads, visits, live forms.

Full description:
Trackbook CRM is the mobile workdesk for field sales teams.

Built for Manager, Team Lead, BDM and Ops users to manage leads, follow-ups, visits and onboarding forms on the go — synced with your company’s Trackbook CRM on the web.

KEY FEATURES
• Live Form Builder sync — Admin updates forms on web; field staff get the latest schema automatically
• Fresh Direct onboarding — create and submit merchant forms without hunting for an existing lead
• My / Team leads — Managers & TLs see team pipeline; BDMs see their own book
• Follow-ups & visits — due today, overdue, complete assigned visits
• Call log, status, product & activity on each lead
• Secure login with role-based access
• Camera / gallery upload for form documents

WHO IT’S FOR
Authorised employees of companies that use Trackbook CRM (Newish Technology). This is not a consumer social app.
